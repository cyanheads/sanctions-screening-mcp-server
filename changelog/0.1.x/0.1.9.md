# 0.1.9 — 2026-08-16

## Added

- **`queryTokenCoverage`** on every `approximate` hit of `sanctions_screen_name` and match of `sanctions_resolve_entity` — `{ covered, total }`, a literal count of the query tokens a candidate explains at the applied score floor. Carried in `structuredContent` and rendered by `format()` as `covers N/M query tokens`; absent on exact/strong. ([#15](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/15))
- **`complete` / `truncated` / `missingEntityLeis`** on `sanctions_trace_ownership` — whether the graph is the full known picture, whether ownership relationships exist past the requested `depth`, and which nodes the relationship corpus publishes but the GLEIF Level 1 mirror does not carry (those nodes show their LEI where a legal name would be). ([#16](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/16))
- **`screeningStatus`** on `sanctions_get_entity` (`screened` / `not_ready`) and `sanctions_trace_ownership` (plus `not_requested`) — an empty `sanctionsHits` from a screen that never ran no longer reads like a completed one. ([#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/17))
- **`sanctionsScreen`** — `totalAvailable`, `totalAvailableBasis`, `hasMore` on each screened `sanctions_trace_ownership` node (capped at ten) and on `sanctions_get_entity`'s cross-reference (capped at twenty-five), reusing the disclosure vocabulary `sanctions_screen_name` established in 0.1.8. ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/18), [#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/19))

## Changed

- `README.md` documents coverage-aware ranking and both tools' disclosure fields; `docs/design.md` records ownership-graph completeness and the unavailable-versus-empty cross-reference distinction under Known Limitations.

## Fixed

- **`runFuzzy()` / `runLeiFuzzy()`** rank admitted approximate candidates by raw score, then query-token coverage, then the identity key — one shared exact query token pinned several candidates at `score: 1.0`, leaving `designationId` / `lei` to decide an order that carries no quality signal. `score` keeps its raw Jaro-Winkler value; coverage is a second measurement beside it, never blended in, and `admitFuzzy` admits exactly the same candidates as before. ([#15](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/15))
- **`runLeiFuzzy()`** reports the coverage of the same alias it reports `matchedName` and `score` for, breaking a same-score alias tie on coverage. ([#15](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/15))
- **`traverse()`** decides `truncated` by probing the boundary nodes one hop further rather than by frontier emptiness, so a traversal that reached the edge of the loaded relationship corpus and a cycle terminated by dedup both report `complete`. Probed relationships never enter the returned graph, which stays bounded by `depth`. ([#16](https://github.com/cyanheads/sanctions-screening-mcp-server/issues/16))
